A focused path through the public AMReye.AI material. Prototype, proposed products and future research remain visibly separate.

Current prototype

Problem

Plate reading needs a clearer record.

The current priority is a bounded endpoint reader: calibrated plate imaging, visible zone measurement, quality checks, uncertainty and qualified review.

Evidence boundary

Workflow

Every value needs an origin.

Product

A modular family, not one oversized promise.

The public project separates the portable reader, connected platform ideas and proposed APEX XR research workstation so each path can earn its own evidence.

Validation workstreams

Roadmap

Validation comes before claims.

The next gates are calibration, repeatability, unreadable-image handling, correction provenance, qualified review and supervised partner pilots.

AMReye.AI Quick Pitch

Timer: 4:00

Current demonstration: AMReye.AI demonstrates a synthetic plate-review workflow and a searchable project library. It does not process patient images or establish clinical performance.

Next steps: Develop and test calibrated imaging, repeatability and correction provenance before any supervised clinical evaluation. Bio-InnoQuest recognition and PU EPIC selection are project milestones, not clinical validation.
