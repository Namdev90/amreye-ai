# AMReye.AI: Antimicrobial Intelligence

This note records the landing-page language and naming conventions approved in the September 2026 site refresh. It supplements the existing technical compendium; it does not revise that PDF or claim new scientific validation.

## Core language

- Use **Antimicrobial Intelligence** for the project direction: connecting microbiological measurements, traceable records, frontier instrumentation and AI.
- Use **AI for antimicrobial resistance** as the purpose statement. Keep AI capitalized, bold and highlighted in the site accent colour.
- Hero: **Microbes. Huge impact.** Supporting ambition: **We're working toward an even greater impact for good.**
- Describe the direction as moving **from plate analysis to a complete microbiological record**. This is a vision, not a claim of a validated end-to-end product.

## Product names and maturity

- **Antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) system prototype from Goa, India.** Reader V1 is the current prototype pathway. AST means antimicrobial susceptibility testing; AMR means antimicrobial resistance.
- **Antimicrobial Intelligence Workstation · APEX XR.** Retain APEX XR. It is an advanced R&D direction and design illustration, not a completed or clinically validated workstation.
- Use **Prototype under development** for the current prototype and **Startup project under incubation** in the footer. These labels do not imply regulatory clearance or clinical readiness.

## Library and navigation

- Public page: **Library**, at `/library`. Preserve incoming `#apex` bookmarks and topic queries through compatibility routing.
- Directory card: **Follow your curiosity.** Body: **The complete technical, microbiological and AI topic library.**
- The current repository contains 150 topics and 121 reference entries, including internal project inputs. Say **150 research & innovation topics**, not **150 proven innovations**. Keep the landing count synchronized with the repository.
- Primary action: **Simulate the workflow**. Keep **Quick pitch** in the header without a duplicate hero pitch button. The demonstrations use synthetic data.
- Workflow: **Trust the process. Follow the evidence.** Collaboration: **Connect with our vision.**
