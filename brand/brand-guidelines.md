# AMReye.AI brand identity

Use the exact spelling AMReye.AI. AMR and AI are uppercase; eye is lowercase. Do not insert a hyphen or use a registration symbol. Direction 01, Aperture, is selected. Directions 02, Convergence, and 03, Observation, are retained as alternatives on the exploration sheet.

The selected Aperture mark joins a circular plate with an observation aperture. SVG files are the scalable originals. PNG variants have transparent backgrounds. Use graphite on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds. Keep at least one pupil diameter clear on every side. For icons below 32 pixels, use favicon.svg with its simpler silhouette.

Colours: teal #087F83, graphite #17252D, silver #B9C8CA, white #FFFFFF. Silver is an accent, not small reading text. The wordmark uses Arial Bold with Helvetica and sans-serif fallbacks. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows or place the mark over scientific measurements.

Website image copies carry a small high-contrast logo watermark in a clear corner. Clean originals are retained separately. Images representing proposed instruments remain labelled conceptual. A watermark establishes visual attribution, not clinical validation or a registered trademark.
